Question: Simplify (x + 2)*(x + 3).
<think>
Using the distributive property (FOIL method):
First: x * x = x^2
Outer: x * 3 = 3*x
Inner: 2 * x = 2*x
Last: 2 * 3 = 6
Summing all terms: x^2 + 3*x + 2*x + 6 = x^2 + 5*x + 6.
</think>
<answer>x^2 + 5*x + 6</answer>
